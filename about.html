Our Story

From a grudge to a formula.

Poison Theory started in 2024 as a reaction — a refusal to accept the thin, overpriced, disposable denim flooding the market. We wanted something heavier. Honest. Built to survive a decade, not a season.

Every piece is cut, stitched, and sealed in small batches in our workshop. No middlemen, no shortcuts, no watered-down formulas. Just raw selvedge, brutal construction, and a label that warns you what you're getting into.
